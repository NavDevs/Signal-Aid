Process

How it works

1
01

Login & Verify

Secure authentication with Driver ID and Vehicle Number, ensuring only authorized personnel map to dispatch units.

2
02

Plan Dispatch

Accept an incoming emergency request. The system maps the route and calculates ETA based on live traffic data.

3
03

Select Criticality

Choose Normal, High, or Critical. This dynamically adjusts signal preemption priority along your corridor.

4
04

Preempt Signals

ML orchestrates traffic signals ahead of you. Intersections shift from red to green automatically as you approach.

Capabilities

System architecture

SIMULATED IN THIS VERSION

Signal Preemption

The core capability. The app simulates communicating with municipal traffic grids to turn upcoming lights green, ensuring zero-stop emergency transit.

NEXT INTERSECTION
Main St & 4th Ave

Dispatch Planning

Optimized real-time route generation based on incident location and live traffic data.

Live Updates

Powered by Socket.IO. New emergency requests are pushed instantly, no manual refresh needed.

Live Response Tracking

View your ETA, active route, and the real-time status of signals ahead in one streamlined view.

Server Protection

Detects backend wipes via dataEpoch, safely signing drivers out to prevent corruption.

Trip History

Full cryptographic logs of responses, ETAs, and completion statuses saved via SharedPreferences.

Priority

Dynamic Response Criticality

Not every call requires clearing the entire grid. Select your priority to manage traffic impact responsibly.

Normal

Standard routing without widespread preemption. Used for non-life-threatening transport.

PREEMPTION RADIUS: 0.5mi

High

Active emergency. Signals clear incrementally as the vehicle approaches each intersection.

PREEMPTION RADIUS: 1.5mi

Critical

Absolute priority. Entire corridors are flushed and locked green for mass-casualty or severe trauma.

PREEMPTION RADIUS: ENTIRE ROUTE
